The Visual Object Tracking (VOT) Challenge Series has received the PAMI Mark Everingham Prize at ECCV 2026, in recognition of its long-standing contribution to the computer vision community.

Since 2013, VOT has brought together the visual tracking community through a continuous series of challenges and workshops, establishing rigorous evaluation methodologies, benchmarks, datasets, and open-source tools. The award recognizes the VOT team and its collaborators *“for the series of Visual Object Tracking workshops and challenges that have shaped the modern synthesis of visual object tracking.”*

We thank all researchers, challenge participants, organizers, and collaborators who have contributed to VOT over the years and made this achievement possible.
