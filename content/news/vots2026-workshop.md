The VOTS2026 workshop was held on September 9, 2026, at ECCV2026 in Copenhagen, Denmark. The workshop featured presentations of the challenge results, invited talks, and discussions on the future of visual object tracking and segmentation research. It was attended by around 70 participants.

**Lost items**: After the workshop the organizers found two pieces of forgotten tech in the room: a MacBook Pro laptop and a ReMarkable tabled. If you are the owner of these items, please contact the organizers to find out how to retrieve them.
