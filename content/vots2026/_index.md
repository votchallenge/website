# VOTS2026 Challenge

Visual Object Tracking and Segmentation challenge VOTS2026 is a continuation of the VOTS2023 and VOTS2024 challenges, which no longer distinct between single- and multi-target tracking nor between short- and long-term tracking. It requires tracking one or more targets simultaneously by segmentation over long or short sequences, while the targets may disappear during tracking and reappear later in the video.


## Four challenges are organized

<div class="card-grid">
<div class="challenge-card"><i class="fa fa-cubes card-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i><div><strong>VOTS2026 Challenge</strong> Continuation of the VOTS2025 challenge with the task to track one or more general targets over short-term or long-term sequences by segmentation. The dataset is refreshed with new challenging sequences.</div></div>
<div class="challenge-card"><i class="fa fa-random card-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i><div><strong>VOTSt2026 Challenge</strong> Continuation of the VOTSt2025 transformative object tracking challenge, which considers general objects undergoing a topological transformation, such as vegetables cut into pieces, machines disassembled, etc.</div></div>
<div class="challenge-card"><i class="fa fa-crosshairs card-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i><div><strong>VOTSp2026 Challenge</strong>A new point-tracking challenge, which considers tracking several manually defined points. Challenging sequences have been selected to construct a new test set for his challenge.</div></div>
<div class="challenge-card"><i class="fa fa-comments card-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i><div><strong>VOTSr2026 Challenge</strong>A new referring tracking challenge, with the task to segment objects in video based on descriptions.</div></div>
</div>
